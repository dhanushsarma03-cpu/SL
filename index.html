A LETTER FROM DHANUSH

My Sri Lekha,

There are so many things I want to tell you, and somehow the simplest one is still the most important:

I love you.

I want to be there for the whole thing — the ups and downs, the smiles and tears, the laughter and the fights, the exciting days and the completely normal ones. I don't want only the beautiful parts of life with you. I want the real version of it.

I want to be able to be myself with you, and I want you to always feel like you can be yourself with me. I want to listen when you need to talk, give you space when you need it, hold your hand when you need someone beside you, and celebrate you when you achieve something you once thought was impossible.

I can't promise that every day will be perfect. But I can promise that I will always try to make our love a safe place — a place where we can laugh, learn, disagree, forgive, grow and choose each other again.

Thank you for becoming such a beautiful part of my life.

Forever yours,
Dhanush ♡
